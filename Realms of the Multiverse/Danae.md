# Sub-regions and Geography

## East and Northeast

The East and Northeastern sub–regions of Danaé are bordered by Aste to the east and the Dark Kingdom to the northeast and northwest. This region is mostly urban and flatlands, consisting of two of the ten most populous metro areas in the region, Astrellia-SF Castle and Amaressio. However, as you get further north towards the Dark Kingdom, more hills are visible.

- Borders: Shadow Lake (E/Aste), Windmere/Lakemere (NE/E), Groge Lake (NE/Aste/Drk. Kingdoms)
- Highest point: Tundell Hill, 560ft.
- Capitol: Astrellia

## Central

This sub–region is the one known most for its farmlands. However, the majority of its northern reaches are hills, and this sub–region's northern half is home to the highest peak in Danaé, Taltor Mountain, standing at 1036ft.

- Borders: Talvert Ridge (Southern half of E), Galsing Plateau (Northern half of E and NE), Diamond River (Dark Kingdom)
- Highest point: Taltor Mountain, 1036ft
- Capital: Asori

## West and Northwest

These are the most varied subregions, from the coastal regions to the tall hills of Gandenzall. It also holds the capital of Danae, Castalia. It's the most varied of the regions in terms of populace too, given that it is the only sub–region of Danae that exhibits magic.

- Borders: Pacific Ocean (W), Talus Park (Central), Nadine (Dark Kingdom)
- Tallest point: Glass Hill, 300ft
- Capital: izah-17

# Self-governing Territories

## [[Realms of the Multiverse/Allied Islands|Allied Islands]]

The Allied Islands are a chain of five major islands plus several more minor ones. While the Allieds are independent from Danae, most of its laws align with that of mainland of Danae.

- Islands: Harboredge, Canderet, Seafoam Isles, College, and Caltoria
- Highest point: College Observatoium Hill (900 ft.)
- Capital: Arret Town

# Basic History

## Pre-Castle

The Matricians came out of their proto-portal into a world not unlike the [[Realms of the Multiverse/Shadow Fields|Shadow Fields]]. It was primitive and dark. Creatures similar to the ones we now know as _Corrupted_ creatures roamed the lands of the Kingdom of Danae, comprised of its subjects' faces. While we don't know much about Danae before the Matricians, based off these initial descriptions life there was hard. Having had to fight through the corruption of [[Realms of the Multiverse/Matrix|the Matrix]], they, along with their friends made in Danae, conquered the Dark Lord responsible for these monsters and restored light to the Castle, now known as _Castle-upon-Akala_, which remains the governmental seat of Danae.

## Maccore and the Second Dark War

For some years, the Castle stood in peace. Maccore, the middle of the three Matricians, was the one chose to lead this new–ish monarchy. Corruption seeds from peace, as the Dark Lord returned. He sapped the power of the Castle, a power similar to the pre–Sundering magic that lent the Castle a power that maintained its occupants.

Thus, the horrid proto–Corrupted creatures were allowed to run rampant once again. They were stronger due to enhancement from the Castle's power, and caused more difficulties for Maccore and company.They prevailed once again and defeated the Dark Lord and restored peace.

## First Expansion– izah's
After much of the Castle was destroyed, they needed a new government to prevent the collapse of Danae again. Thusly, they dismantled the monarchy that Maccore had been and formed the Kahunaite, with Maccore as the leader. The newly–formed governance then expanded past ther small lordship they had control over. They named each city izah, with a number indicating the time at which it was founded. 1 and 2 were realitivly well–founded in the sense that trade flowed betwen them, but expansion was soon halted in favor of expanding the trade of these cities. But peace never lasts, and the darkness landed on Danae once again.
